The trading framework

01

Higher TF Analysis

Mark structure, important levels and areas where liquidity may be resting.

02

Liquidity

Wait for a meaningful liquidity sweep or liquidity-based setup instead of chasing price.

03

Execution

Use a lower timeframe for precise entries, with defined invalidation and risk before entry.

04

Journal

Record the setup, execution, result and lesson. The goal is process improvement, not revenge trading.

Risk statement

Respect the risk. Protect the account.

Trading is uncertain. A losing trade is part of the process; breaking the plan is the real mistake.
